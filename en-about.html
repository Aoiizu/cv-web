Profile

Japan, told through
photography

My name is Aoi. I'm a photographer and frontend developer traveling between Okinawa and Hokkaido. I continue to document Japan's seasons and nature through both film and digital.

This site is not a portfolio of "best work" — it's an honest archive of every place I've visited and everything the light has shown me. Each frame still carries the air and silence of that day.

Philosophy

Not searching for the perfect frame, but leaving proof that I was there. Light never appears the same way twice. So I stop, I look, and I press the shutter. That alone is enough.
